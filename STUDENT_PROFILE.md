## 📋 Executive Summary

This **Scientific AI Engineering Curriculum** is a production-ready, open-source educational resource designed for intermediate to advanced learners who want to master the intersection of Physics-Informed Machine Learning, Building Performance Simulation, and Production AI Systems.

**Who This Curriculum Is For:**
- 🎓 Graduate students (Master's/PhD) in Engineering, CS, or Architecture
- 🔬 Researchers in building simulation, optimization, or AI/ML
- 💼 Professional engineers transitioning to AI-powered systems
- 🏢 Organizations training teams in scientific computing
- 📚 Educators teaching advanced ML engineering courses

**Key Requirement:** Strong foundation in programming and willingness to learn. No prior BPS or AI experience required, but accelerates learning significantly.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### "I don't have an engineering degree. Can I still do this?"

**Answer:** Yes, if you have strong programming skills and motivation. The physics and domain knowledge are taught from scratch. However, expect to spend more time on Months 1-4.

### "I'm a senior software engineer but have zero physics knowledge. Should I start?"

**Answer:** Absolutely. Your coding skills will accelerate learning. Focus preparation on physics basics (1-2 weeks) and you'll excel.

### "Can I do this part-time while working full-time?"

**Answer:** Yes, but it's challenging. Budget 6-8 hours per week for an extended 18-24 month timeline. Weekend blocks work best.

### "What if I get stuck?"

**Answer:** 
1. Review prerequisites for that month in exercise files
2. Consult troubleshooting guides (available for Months 7+)
3. Use ChatGPT/Copilot for concept clarification
4. Ideally have a mentor for 1 hour/week Q&A
5. Join online communities (GitHub Issues, Stack Overflow)

### "Do I need to complete it in 12 months?"

**Answer:** No. The curriculum is self-paced. Most complete in 12-18 months. Take longer if needed—quality over speed.

### "Can I skip months or do them out of order?"

**Answer:** **No skipping Month 0** (mandatory). Months 1-12 build on each other. Skipping creates knowledge gaps. Follow sequentially.

### "Is there a certificate?"

**Answer:** Not formally, but completing the capstone project serves as a portfolio piece. Your GitHub repository with all completed exercises demonstrates mastery better than any certificate.
