## 📝 Submission Text

### **About This Project**

This is a **comprehensive, production-ready, open-source curriculum** developed at the University of São Paulo (USP) with support from FAPESP (São Paulo Research Foundation). It's designed as an **Open Educational Resource (OER)** for teaching advanced Scientific AI Engineering applied to Building Performance Simulation.

**Primary Content:** Portuguese (with complementary English materials)  
**License:** MIT (freely available for educational, research, and commercial use)
