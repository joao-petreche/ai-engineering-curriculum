   - 6 operational buildings in Singapore tropical climate
   - Sub-hourly (15-min to 1-hour) measurements
   - 📝 Nature Scientific Data: DOI 10.1038/s41597-023-02525-3
   - Variables: Energy, temperature, humidity, HVAC, weather
   - Perfect for addressing "reality gap" with real IoT sensors

🥉 #3: I-BLEND (India Campus Dataset)
   - Campus-scale commercial and residential buildings
   - Sub-hourly to hourly measurements
   - 📝 Nature Scientific Data: DOI 10.1038/s41597-022-01721-4
   - Indian hot-humid climate zones
   - Designed specifically for ML applications
